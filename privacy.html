No data collection

XML to FCPXML does not collect, store or send any personal data. All conversion happens on your Mac. The XML files and media you open are read only to convert them and are never uploaded anywhere.

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and makes no network connections.

Feedback

If you email feedback or send it through TestFlight, your message and any files you attach are used only to answer you and fix the problem, and are deleted afterwards.
